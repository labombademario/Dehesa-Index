README| USDA Agricultural Projection tables contain data covering 1 year of 
README| history (2024), the current year, and projections for the next 10 years through 2035 of 
README| the supply and use of selected U.S. crop and animal commodities.  
README| 
README|  - Variable names are contained in the first row: 
README| 	Attribute......(Supply and use category variable name) (String)

README| 	Commoditytype..(One of two categories: Crop or 
README| 			Livestock/Dairy) (String)
README| 	Units..........(Measurement unit) (String)
README| 	Year type......(Either marketing year or calendar year) (String)
README| 	Year...........(Year of measurement within the current projection 
README| 			year) (String)
README| 	Value..........(Data value) (Double) 
README| 
README|  - Marketing years differ by commodity.  
README| 	Corn....................September 1 
README| 	Sorghum.................September 1
README| 	Barley..................June 1
README| 	Oats....................June 1
README| 	Wheat...................June 1
README| 	Soybeans................September 1

README| 	Soybean meal............October 1

README| 	Rice, California........October 1
