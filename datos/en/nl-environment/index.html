Dehesa Index brings together 35 “Manure and environment” series for Netherlands, with official data and its history (since Jan 1950, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 5 of the 15 series rose, 10 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Pérdida de nitrógeno al suelo y al agua, Países Bajos (CBS)” (+21.6 %, 2024); the largest fall, “Eficiencia de uso del nitrógeno, Países Bajos (CBS)” (−5.88 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Pérdida de nitrógeno al suelo y al agua, Países Bajos (CBS)” rose most (+21.5 %) and “Eficiencia de uso del nitrógeno, Países Bajos (CBS)” fell most (−5.88 %). “Excedente de nitrógeno de la ganadería, Países Bajos (CBS)” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 1990); 5 series are at their lowest on record.
