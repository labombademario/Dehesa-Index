In brief

In their latest reading, 2 of the 15 series rose, 10 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Organic production: Potatoes (including seed potatoes) (tonnes, Eurostat)” (+4.87 %, 2024); the largest fall, “Organic production: Common wheat and spelt (tonnes, Eurostat)” (−36.0 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Organic production: Potatoes (including seed potatoes) (tonnes, Eurostat)” rose most (+4.87 %) and “Organic production: Common wheat and spelt (tonnes, Eurostat)” fell most (−36.0 %).
