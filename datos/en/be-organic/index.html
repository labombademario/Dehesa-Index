Organic farming — Belgium

Dehesa Index brings together 29 “Organic farming” series for Belgium, with official data and its history (since Jan 2016, latest 2024). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 6 of the 12 series rose, 6 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Organic production: Grain maize and corn-cob-mix (tonnes, Eurostat)” (+37.1 %, 2024); the largest fall, “Organic production: Cereals for the production of grain (including seed) (tonnes, Eurostat)” (−25.4 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (12 comparable series), “Organic production: Grain maize and corn-cob-mix (tonnes, Eurostat)” rose most (+37.1 %) and “Organic production: Cereals for the production of grain (including seed) (tonnes, Eurostat)” fell most (−25.4 %).
