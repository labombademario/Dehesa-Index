Organic farming — Belgium

Dehesa Index brings together 41 “Organic farming” series for Belgium, with official data and its history (since Jan 2012, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 4 of the 14 series rose, 9 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Organic slaughter: Bovine meat (Tonne, Eurostat)” (+26.7 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Organic production: Cereals for the production of grain (including seed) (tonnes, Eurostat)” (−25.4 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (14 comparable series), “Organic slaughter: Bovine meat (Tonne, Eurostat)” rose most (+26.7 %) and “Organic production: Cereals for the production of grain (including seed) (tonnes, Eurostat)” fell most (−25.4 %).
