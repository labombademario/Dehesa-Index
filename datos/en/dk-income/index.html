Dehesa Index brings together 61 “Farm receipts and income” series for Denmark, with official data and its history (since Jan 1973, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 10 of the 15 series rose, 5 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Oats and summer cereal mixtures (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (+68.2 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Protein crops (including seeds) (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (−18.2 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Oats and summer cereal mixtures (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” rose most (+68.2 %) and “Protein crops (including seeds) (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” fell most (−18.2 %). “Animals (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 1973); 2 series are at their lowest on record.
