In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 12 series rose, 4 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Agricultural land price: arable land (EUR/ha, Eurostat; as reported by Portugal, far above other EU countries and not cross-checked with a national source)” (+11.3 %, 2024); the largest fall, “Electricity price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption from 20 MWh to 499 MWh, band IB (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” (−2.92 %, 2026-S1). Against the same period a year earlier (12 comparable series), “Agricultural land price: arable land (EUR/ha, Eurostat; as reported by Portugal, far above other EU countries and not cross-checked with a national source)” rose most (+11.3 %) and “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption from 1 000 GJ to 9 999 GJ -band I2 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” fell most (−14.0 %).
