Organic farming — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 25 “Organic farming” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 2006, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 15 series rose, 5 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Organic agricultural area under conversion (GUS)” (+24.2 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Organic processors: Manufacture of dairy products (Number, Eurostat)” (−26.7 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Organic agricultural area under conversion (GUS)” rose most (+24.2 %) and “Organic processors: Manufacture of dairy products (Number, Eurostat)” fell most (−26.7 %).
