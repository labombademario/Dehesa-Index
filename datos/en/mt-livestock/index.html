Livestock census — Malta

Dehesa Index brings together 48 “Livestock census” series for Malta, with official data and its history (since Jan 1987, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 3 of the 14 series rose, 11 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Male calves, less than 1 year old, not for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” (+8.70 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Male bovine animals, 1 to less than 2 years old (November, December, Eurostat)” (−37.4 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (14 comparable series), “Male calves, less than 1 year old, not for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” rose most (+8.70 %) and “Male bovine animals, 1 to less than 2 years old (November, December, Eurostat)” fell most (−37.4 %). 6 series are at their lowest on record.
