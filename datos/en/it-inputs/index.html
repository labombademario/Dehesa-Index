In brief

In their latest reading, 13 of the 15 series rose, 1 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+17.0 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption less than 1 000 GJ, band I1 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” (−5.15 %, 2026-S1).
