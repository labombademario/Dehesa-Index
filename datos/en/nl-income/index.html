In brief

In their latest reading, 5 of the 15 series rose, 10 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Other cereals (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (+109 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Protein crops (including seeds) (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (−22.1 %, 2025).
