Dehesa Index brings together 103 “Livestock census” series for Germany, with official data and its history (since Jan 1959, latest Aug 2026). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 3 of the 15 series rose, 12 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Female calves, less than 1 year old, not for slaughter (May-June, Eurostat)” (+4.85 %, 2026); the largest fall, “Chicks hatched, goslings, Germany” (−57.4 %, Aug 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Chicks hatched, turkey poults, Germany” rose most (+5.89 %) and “Heifers, 2 years old or over, for slaughter (May-June, Eurostat)” fell most (−10.6 %). 3 series are at their lowest on record.
