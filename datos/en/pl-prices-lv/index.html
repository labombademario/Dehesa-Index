Livestock prices — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 22 “Livestock prices” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 1999, latest Aug 2026). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 12 of the 15 series rose, 3 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Calves for slaughter (live weight): procurement price, annual average (GUS)” (+30.9 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Fresh eggs (whole country) (producer price, Eurostat)” (−14.1 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Calves for slaughter (live weight): procurement price, annual average (GUS)” rose most (+30.9 %) and “Pigs for slaughter (live weight): procurement price, monthly (GUS)” fell most (−14.6 %). 6 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
