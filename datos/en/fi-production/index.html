In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 15 series rose, 6 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Calf and young cattley: slaughterings, monthly (head, Eurostat)” (+100 %, Aug 2026); the largest fall, “Meat production (slaughterhouses): fattening pigs, Finland (Luke)” (−10.8 %, Aug 2026).
