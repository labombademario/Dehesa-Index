Farm receipts and income — Italy

Dehesa Index brings together 229 “Farm receipts and income” series for Italy, with official data and its history (since Jan 1980, latest 2026-Q2). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 15 series rose, 6 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Trentino-Alto Adige” (+33.4 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Lombardia” (−22.7 %, 2026-Q2). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Marche” rose most (+34.8 %) and “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Veneto” fell most (−26.3 %). “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Valle d'Aosta” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 2018); “Employed in agriculture, forestry and fishing (Labour Force Survey, thousand persons): Veneto” is at its lowest (since 2018).
