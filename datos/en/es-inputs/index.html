Input prices — Spain

Dehesa Index brings together 25 “Input prices” series for Spain, with official data and its history (since Jan 2002, latest 2026-S1). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 13 of the 15 series rose, 1 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+25.0 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Electricity (2020=100, Eurostat)” (−7.11 %, 2026-Q2). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” rose most (+44.8 %) and “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption from 1 000 GJ to 9 999 GJ -band I2 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” fell most (−11.1 %). 2 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
