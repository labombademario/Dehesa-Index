In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 14 series rose, 5 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Skimmed milk powder: production (monthly, Eurostat)” (+18.2 %, Aug 2026); the largest fall, “Butter, incl. dehydrated butter and ghee, and other fats and oils derived from milk; dairy spreads: production (monthly, Eurostat)” (−33.7 %, Aug 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (14 comparable series), “Acidified milk (yoghurts and other): production (monthly, Eurostat)” rose most (+26.3 %) and “Milk and cream powders, excluding skimmed milk powders: production (monthly, Eurostat)” fell most (−50.0 %). 3 series are at the highest level in their whole history; “Milk and cream powders, excluding skimmed milk powders: production (monthly, Eurostat)” is at its lowest (since 1973).
