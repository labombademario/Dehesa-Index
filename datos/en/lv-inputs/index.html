In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 14 series rose, 5 fell and 1 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+26.1 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Plant protection products (2020=100, Eurostat)” (−30.3 %, 2026-Q2).
