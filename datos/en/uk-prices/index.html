In brief

In their latest reading, 5 of the 15 series rose, 7 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Producer price, United Kingdom: prime cattle (pence per kg deadweight)” (+30.2 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Farm-gate price, United Kingdom: Cauliflowers (GBP per tonne)” (−13.8 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Consumer price inflation (CPIH), United Kingdom: food (annual change, %)” rose most (+125 %) and “Farm-gate price, United Kingdom: Cauliflowers (GBP per tonne)” fell most (−13.8 %). 4 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
