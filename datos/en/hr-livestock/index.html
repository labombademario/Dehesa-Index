Dehesa Index brings together 48 “Livestock census” series for Croatia, with official data and its history (since Jan 2000, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 4 of the 15 series rose, 11 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Heifers, 1 year old, for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” (+11.1 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Bovine animals, less than 1 year old, for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” (−4.21 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Heifers, 1 year old, for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” rose most (+11.1 %) and “Bovine animals, less than 1 year old, for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” fell most (−4.21 %). “Heifers, 1 year old, for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 2000); “Female bovine animals, 2 years old or over (November, December, Eurostat)” is at its lowest (since 2000).
