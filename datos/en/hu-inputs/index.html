Dehesa Index brings together 34 “Input prices” series for Hungary, with official data and its history (since Jan 1990, latest 2026-S1). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 7 of the 15 series rose, 5 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption less than 1 000 GJ, band I1 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” (+17.8 %, 2026-S1); the largest fall, “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Fuels for heating (2020=100, Eurostat)” (−4.38 %, 2026-Q2). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption less than 1 000 GJ, band I1 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” rose most (+19.4 %) and “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption from 1 000 GJ to 9 999 GJ -band I2 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” fell most (−3.13 %). “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Lubricants and other energy products than electricity, and heating and motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 2020); 2 series are at their lowest on record.
