Livestock census — Estonia

Dehesa Index brings together 38 “Livestock census” series for Estonia, with official data and its history (since Jan 1987, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 15 series rose, 6 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Male calves, less than 1 year old, not for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” (+5.16 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Breeding sows, live weight 50 kg or over (November, December, Eurostat)” (−41.8 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Male calves, less than 1 year old, not for slaughter (November, December, Eurostat)” rose most (+5.16 %) and “Breeding sows, live weight 50 kg or over (November, December, Eurostat)” fell most (−41.8 %). 4 series are at their lowest on record.
