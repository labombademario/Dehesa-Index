In brief

In their latest reading, 4 of the 15 series rose, 8 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Area under agri-environment schemes: United Kingdom (hectares)” (+13.1 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Area under agri-environment schemes: Wales (hectares)” (−32.2 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (14 comparable series), “Area under agri-environment schemes: United Kingdom (hectares)” rose most (+13.1 %) and “Fertiliser use, Great Britain: nitrogen (N) on grassland (kg per hectare)” fell most (−10.5 %). 6 series are at their lowest on record.
