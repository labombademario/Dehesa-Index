Dehesa Index brings together 47 “Input prices” series for Canada, with official data and its history (since Jan 1990, latest Sep 2026). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 12 of the 15 series rose, 1 fell and 2 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Alberta farm input prices: Diesel Fuel, marked farm fuel, (provincial allowance deducted), 100 litres” (+26.4 %, Aug 2026); the largest fall, “Alberta farm input prices: Fence Posts, standard grade, treated, 3-4" diameter, 7' length” (−7.82 %, Aug 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Retail price: diesel (self-service), Toronto (monthly)” rose most (+82.3 %) and “Alberta farm input prices: Baler Twine, plastic, for round baler, 28,000' bale” fell most (−20.1 %). 9 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
