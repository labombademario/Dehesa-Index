Manure and environment — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 30 “Manure and environment” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 1999, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 11 of the 15 series rose, 4 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Electricity consumption, households (GUS)” (+13.4 %, 2024); the largest fall, “Electricity production, water power plants (GUS)” (−16.3 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Electricity consumption, households (GUS)” rose most (+13.4 %) and “Electricity production, water power plants (GUS)” fell most (−16.3 %). 2 series are at the highest level in their whole history; “Electricity production, thermal power plants (GUS)” is at its lowest (since 2000).
