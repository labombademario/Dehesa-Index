Dehesa Index brings together 13 “Livestock census” series for Switzerland, with official data and its history (since Jan 2001, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 13 series rose, 4 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Domestic table eggs, total (FOAG)” (+7.30 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Rabbit meat: market supply, domestic production plus imports (FOAG)” (−14.2 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (13 comparable series), “Domestic table eggs, total (FOAG)” rose most (+7.30 %) and “Rabbit meat: market supply, domestic production plus imports (FOAG)” fell most (−14.2 %). 2 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
