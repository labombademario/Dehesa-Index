Input prices — Netherlands

Dehesa Index brings together 25 “Input prices” series for Netherlands, with official data and its history (since 2007-S1, latest 2026-S1). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 11 of the 15 series rose, 2 fell and 2 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Energy; lubricants (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+16.7 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Electricity (2020=100, Eurostat)” (−2.52 %, 2026-Q2). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” rose most (+40.1 %) and “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Fuels for heating (2020=100, Eurostat)” fell most (−6.94 %). 3 series are at the highest level in their whole history.
