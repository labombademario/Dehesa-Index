Input prices — Slovenia

Dehesa Index brings together 15 “Input prices” series for Slovenia, with official data and its history (since 2007-S2, latest 2026-S1). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 15 series rose, 4 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Fuels for heating (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+31.4 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Natural gas price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption less than 1 000 GJ, band I1 (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” (−5.06 %, 2026-S1). Against the same period a year earlier (14 comparable series), “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Fuels for heating (2020=100, Eurostat)” rose most (+30.8 %) and “Electricity price for non-household consumers, excluding VAT: Consumption from 20 MWh to 499 MWh, band IB (Kilowatt-hour, Eurostat)” fell most (−4.97 %). 2 series are at the highest level in their whole history; “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Lubricants and other energy products than electricity, and heating and motor fuels (2020=100, Eurostat)” is at its lowest (since 2020).
