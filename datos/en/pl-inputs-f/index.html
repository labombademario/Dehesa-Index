Fertiliser prices paid — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 12 “Fertiliser prices paid” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 2000, latest 2026-Q2). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 12 series rose, 4 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Nitrogenous fertilisers (2020=100, Eurostat)” (+0.46 %, 2026-Q2); the largest fall, “Consumption of inorganic fertilisers: Nitrogen (tonnes of nutrient, Eurostat)” (−9.21 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (12 comparable series), “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: NP fertilisers (2020=100, Eurostat)” rose most (+0.56 %) and “Consumption of inorganic fertilisers: Nitrogen (tonnes of nutrient, Eurostat)” fell most (−9.21 %). “Input price index (prices paid by farmers), nominal: Other fertilisers, soil improvers (2020=100, Eurostat)” is at the highest level in its whole series (since 2020); “Consumption of inorganic fertilisers: Phosphorus (tonnes of nutrient, Eurostat)” is at its lowest (since 2000).
