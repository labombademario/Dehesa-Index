In brief

In their latest reading, 8 of the 15 series rose, 7 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Slaughterings at abattoirs: calves (1,000 head), Sweden (Jordbruksverket)” (+41.4 %, Aug 2026); the largest fall, “Slaughterings at abattoirs: sows (1,000 head), Sweden (Jordbruksverket)” (−15.8 %, Aug 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Slaughterings at abattoirs: sows (1,000 head), Sweden (Jordbruksverket)” rose most (+21.7 %) and “Slaughterings at abattoirs: calves (1,000 head), Sweden (Jordbruksverket)” fell most (−6.82 %). 2 series are at their lowest on record.
