Dehesa Index brings together 224 “Production and slaughter” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 1961, latest 2026-S1). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 13 of the 15 series rose, 2 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Cow: slaughterings, monthly (tonnes carcass weight, Eurostat)” (+14.7 %, Jul 2026); the largest fall, “Bull: slaughterings, monthly (tonnes carcass weight, Eurostat)” (−7.55 %, Jul 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Lamb: slaughterings, monthly (head, Eurostat)” rose most (+14.3 %) and “Chicken: slaughterings, monthly (tonnes carcass weight, Eurostat)” fell most (−21.5 %).
