Livestock census — Latvia

Dehesa Index brings together 46 “Livestock census” series for Latvia, with official data and its history (since Jan 1987, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 5 of the 15 series rose, 10 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Piglets, live weight of under 20 kg (November, December, Eurostat)” (+10.2 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Non milk ewes and ewe-lambs put to the ram (November, December, Eurostat)” (−12.9 %, 2024). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Piglets, live weight of under 20 kg (November, December, Eurostat)” rose most (+10.2 %) and “Non milk ewes and ewe-lambs put to the ram (November, December, Eurostat)” fell most (−12.9 %). 3 series are at their lowest on record.
