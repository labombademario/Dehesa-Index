Farm receipts and income — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 124 “Farm receipts and income” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 1996, latest 2025). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 9 of the 15 series rose, 3 fell and 3 were unchanged against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Oilseeds and oleaginous fruit (including seeds) (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (+26.0 %, 2025); the largest fall, “Sugar beet (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” (−31.7 %, 2025). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Oilseeds and oleaginous fruit (including seeds) (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” rose most (+26.0 %) and “Sugar beet (EUR million, current prices, Eurostat)” fell most (−31.7 %). 2 series are at their lowest on record.
