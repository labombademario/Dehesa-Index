Livestock census — Poland

Dehesa Index brings together 114 “Livestock census” series for Poland, with official data and its history (since Jan 1987, latest 2026). Each value is shown as the source publishes it, not converted or recalculated.

In brief

In their latest reading, 6 of the 15 series rose, 9 fell against the previous observation. The largest rise is “Bovine animals, less than 1 year old, for slaughter (May-June, Eurostat)” (+28.9 %, 2026); the largest fall, “Male bovine animals, 2 years old or over (May-June, Eurostat)” (−25.0 %, 2026). Against the same period a year earlier (15 comparable series), “Bovine animals, less than 1 year old, for slaughter (May-June, Eurostat)” rose most (+28.9 %) and “Male bovine animals, 2 years old or over (May-June, Eurostat)” fell most (−25.0 %). 3 series are at the highest level in their whole history; 2 series are at their lowest on record.
