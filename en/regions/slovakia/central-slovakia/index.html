Central Slovakia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Slovakia (Slovakia): agricultural industry output 451 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Slovakia reached 451 M EUR in 2024 (−5.4 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 39 %, animals (livestock sold) 18 %, animal products 36 % of output; the largest single item is milk (25 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 424 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (36.7 thousand ha, 9 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 5,210 holdings (2023) with an average area of 80.6 ha. Central Slovakia is at rank 3 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
