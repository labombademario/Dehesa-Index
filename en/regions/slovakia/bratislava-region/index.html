Bratislava Region: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Bratislava Region (Slovakia): agricultural industry output 242 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Bratislava Region reached 242 M EUR in 2024 (+8.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 56 %, animals (livestock sold) 5 %, animal products 24 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (16 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 79.4 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (12.1 thousand ha, 15 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 640 holdings (2023) with an average area of 106.1 ha. Bratislava Region is at rank 4 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
