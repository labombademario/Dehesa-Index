Western Slovakia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Slovakia (Slovakia): agricultural industry output 1,634 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Slovakia reached 1,634 M EUR in 2024 (−5.0 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 60 %, animals (livestock sold) 17 %, animal products 16 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (29 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 802 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (153 thousand ha, 19 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 6,970 holdings (2023) with an average area of 114.9 ha. Western Slovakia is at rank 1 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
