Capital Region (Vilnius): farm data and statistics

Official figures for Capital Region (Vilnius) (Lithuania): agricultural industry output 395 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Capital Region (Vilnius) reached 395 M EUR in 2023 (+1.9 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 47 %, animals (livestock sold) 43 %, animal products 8 % of output; the largest single item is poultry (38 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 283 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (58.7 thousand ha, 21 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 26.0, pigs 15.7, sheep 19.4. There are 16,540 holdings (2020) with an average area of 17.1 ha.
