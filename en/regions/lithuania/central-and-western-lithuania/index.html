Central and Western Lithuania: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central and Western Lithuania (Lithuania): agricultural industry output 3,984 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central and Western Lithuania reached 3,984 M EUR in 2023 (−19.2 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 68 %, animals (livestock sold) 14 %, animal products 17 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (36 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 2,575 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (778 thousand ha, 30 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 575, pigs 464, sheep 99.2. There are 115,540 holdings (2020) with an average area of 22.8 ha.
