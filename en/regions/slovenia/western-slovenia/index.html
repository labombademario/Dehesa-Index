Western Slovenia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Slovenia (Slovenia): agricultural industry output 489 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Slovenia reached 489 M EUR in 2023. By origin, crop output 54 %, animals (livestock sold) 21 %, animal products 23 % of output; the largest single item is forage plants (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 142 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is green maize (8.91 thousand ha, 6 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 135, pigs 36.0, sheep 36.1. There are 21,310 holdings (2020) with an average area of 6.6 ha.
