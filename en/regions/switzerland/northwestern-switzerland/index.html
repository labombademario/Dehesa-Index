Northwestern Switzerland: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Northwestern Switzerland (Switzerland): agricultural industry output 1,028 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Northwestern Switzerland reached 1,028 M EUR in 2024 (+1.2 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 40 %, animals (livestock sold) 26 %, animal products 26 % of output; the largest single item is milk (20 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 80.7 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (10.8 thousand ha, 13 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 112, pigs 94.3. There are 4,260 holdings (2016) with an average area of 19.2 ha. Northwestern Switzerland is at rank 5 of 7 for agricultural output among the major regions with data.
