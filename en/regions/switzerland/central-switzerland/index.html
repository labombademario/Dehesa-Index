Central Switzerland: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Switzerland (Switzerland): agricultural industry output 1,705 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Switzerland reached 1,705 M EUR in 2024 (+4.3 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 17 %, animals (livestock sold) 42 %, animal products 34 % of output; the largest single item is milk (31 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 181 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is green maize (6.29 thousand ha, 3 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 251, pigs 438. There are 8,490 holdings (2016) with an average area of 15.4 ha. Central Switzerland is at rank 4 of 7 for agricultural output among the major regions with data.
