West: farm data and statistics

Official figures for West (Romania): agricultural industry output 2,372 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in West reached 2,372 M EUR in 2023 (+7.9 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 62 %, animals (livestock sold) 20 %, animal products 16 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 1,394 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (244 thousand ha, 18 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 162, pigs 834, sheep 1,649. There are 197,640 holdings (2023) with an average area of 7.4 ha. West is at rank 7 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
