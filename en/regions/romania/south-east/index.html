South-East: farm data and statistics

Official figures for South-East (Romania): agricultural industry output 3,368 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in South-East reached 3,368 M EUR in 2023 (−2.3 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 72 %, animals (livestock sold) 11 %, animal products 13 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (28 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 2,057 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (487 thousand ha, 24 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 173, pigs 485, sheep 1,537. There are 321,530 holdings (2023) with an average area of 6.8 ha. South-East is at rank 3 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
