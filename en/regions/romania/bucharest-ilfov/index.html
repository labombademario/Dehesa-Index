Bucharest-Ilfov: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Bucharest-Ilfov (Romania): agricultural industry output 423 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Bucharest-Ilfov reached 423 M EUR in 2023 (+7.0 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 34 %, animals (livestock sold) 1 %, animal products 2 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (18 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 66.7 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (20.2 thousand ha, 30 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 3.00, pigs 5.30, sheep 9.50. There are 16,890 holdings (2023) with an average area of 4.2 ha. Bucharest-Ilfov is at rank 8 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
