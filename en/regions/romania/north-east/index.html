North-East: farm data and statistics

Official figures for North-East (Romania): agricultural industry output 3,667 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in North-East reached 3,667 M EUR in 2023 (+7.0 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 62 %, animals (livestock sold) 16 %, animal products 21 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (19 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 1,843 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (418 thousand ha, 23 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 385, pigs 362, sheep 1,444. There are 590,540 holdings (2023) with an average area of 3.1 ha. North-East is at rank 2 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
