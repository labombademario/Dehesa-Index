North-West: farm data and statistics

Official figures for North-West (Romania): agricultural industry output 2,841 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in North-West reached 2,841 M EUR in 2023 (+9.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 64 %, animals (livestock sold) 13 %, animal products 22 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (18 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 1,720 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (243 thousand ha, 14 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 383, pigs 486, sheep 1,926. There are 434,410 holdings (2023) with an average area of 4.0 ha. North-West is at rank 4 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
