Eastern Central Sweden: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Eastern Central Sweden (Sweden): agricultural industry output 1,515 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Eastern Central Sweden reached 1,515 M EUR in 2024 (+4.4 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 49 %, animals (livestock sold) 25 %, animal products 21 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (20 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 769 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (189 thousand ha, 25 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 10,860 holdings (2023) with an average area of 71.1 ha. Eastern Central Sweden is at rank 3 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
