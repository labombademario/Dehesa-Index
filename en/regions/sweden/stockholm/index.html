Stockholm: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Stockholm (Sweden): agricultural industry output 196 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Stockholm reached 196 M EUR in 2024 (−0.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 60 %, animals (livestock sold) 26 %, animal products 10 % of output; the largest single item is forage plants (24 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 87.7 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (16.7 thousand ha, 19 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 1,830 holdings (2023) with an average area of 47.1 ha. Stockholm is at rank 8 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
