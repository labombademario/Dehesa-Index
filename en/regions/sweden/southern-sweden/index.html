Southern Sweden: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southern Sweden (Sweden): agricultural industry output 1,929 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southern Sweden reached 1,929 M EUR in 2024 (+9.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 64 %, animals (livestock sold) 20 %, animal products 12 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (24 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 526 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (116 thousand ha, 22 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 8,410 holdings (2023) with an average area of 62.6 ha. Southern Sweden is at rank 1 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
