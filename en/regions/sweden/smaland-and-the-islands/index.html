Småland and the islands: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Småland and the islands (Sweden): agricultural industry output 1,294 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Småland and the islands reached 1,294 M EUR in 2024 (+3.8 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 32 %, animals (livestock sold) 26 %, animal products 35 % of output; the largest single item is milk (31 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 495 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (37.0 thousand ha, 7 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 8,900 holdings (2023) with an average area of 55.8 ha. Småland and the islands is at rank 4 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
