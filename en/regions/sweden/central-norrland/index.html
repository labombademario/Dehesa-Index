Central Norrland: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Norrland (Sweden): agricultural industry output 199 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Norrland reached 199 M EUR in 2024 (+4.9 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 32 %, animals (livestock sold) 21 %, animal products 39 % of output; the largest single item is milk (38 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 101 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is barley (3.69 thousand ha, 4 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 3,010 holdings (2023) with an average area of 33.3 ha. Central Norrland is at rank 7 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
