Northern Central Sweden: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Northern Central Sweden (Sweden): agricultural industry output 447 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Northern Central Sweden reached 447 M EUR in 2024 (+3.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 44 %, animals (livestock sold) 24 %, animal products 25 % of output; the largest single item is forage plants (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 253 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is barley (22.5 thousand ha, 9 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 6,430 holdings (2023) with an average area of 39.2 ha. Northern Central Sweden is at rank 5 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
