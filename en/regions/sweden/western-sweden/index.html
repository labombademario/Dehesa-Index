Western Sweden: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Sweden (Sweden): agricultural industry output 1,670 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Sweden reached 1,670 M EUR in 2024 (+4.9 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 42 %, animals (livestock sold) 27 %, animal products 24 % of output; the largest single item is milk (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 643 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (100 thousand ha, 16 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 13,490 holdings (2023) with an average area of 47.7 ha. Western Sweden is at rank 2 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
