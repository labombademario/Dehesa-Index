Northern Croatia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Northern Croatia (Croatia): agricultural industry output 828 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Northern Croatia reached 828 M EUR in 2023 (−2.5 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 48 %, animals (livestock sold) 35 %, animal products 15 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (17 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 234 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (90.9 thousand ha, 39 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 104, pigs 241, sheep 35.3. There are 40,720 holdings (2023) with an average area of 5.8 ha. Northern Croatia is at rank 2 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
