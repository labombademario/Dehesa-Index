Adriatic Croatia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Adriatic Croatia (Croatia): agricultural industry output 451 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Adriatic Croatia reached 451 M EUR in 2023 (−2.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 65 %, animals (livestock sold) 20 %, animal products 8 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 436 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (20.1 thousand ha, 5 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 73.6, pigs 10.9, sheep 272. There are 45,030 holdings (2023) with an average area of 5.2 ha. Adriatic Croatia is at rank 3 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
