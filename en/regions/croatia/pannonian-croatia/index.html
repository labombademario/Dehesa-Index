Pannonian Croatia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Pannonian Croatia (Croatia): agricultural industry output 1,536 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Pannonian Croatia reached 1,536 M EUR in 2023 (−19.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 59 %, animals (livestock sold) 29 %, animal products 8 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (24 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 787 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (179 thousand ha, 23 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 239, pigs 644, sheep 213. There are 58,710 holdings (2023) with an average area of 13.1 ha. Pannonian Croatia is at rank 1 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
