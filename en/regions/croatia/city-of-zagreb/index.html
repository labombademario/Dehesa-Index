City of Zagreb: farm data and statistics

Official figures for City of Zagreb (Croatia): agricultural industry output 25.8 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in City of Zagreb reached 25.8 M EUR in 2023 (+12.8 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 58 %, animals (livestock sold) 29 %, animal products 11 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 10.8 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (2.21 thousand ha, 20 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 1.70, pigs 5.00, sheep 1.00. There are 2,360 holdings (2023) with an average area of 3.9 ha. City of Zagreb is at rank 4 of 4 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
