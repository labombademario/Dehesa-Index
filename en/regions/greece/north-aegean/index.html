North Aegean: farm data and statistics

Official figures for North Aegean (Greece): agricultural industry output 214 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in North Aegean reached 214 M EUR in 2023 (+16.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 59 %, animals (livestock sold) 10 %, animal products 28 % of output; the largest single item is milk (25 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 193 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (62.4 thousand ha, 32 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 4.29, pigs 1.79, sheep 383. There are 22,980 holdings (2020) with an average area of 5.4 ha. North Aegean is at rank 11 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
