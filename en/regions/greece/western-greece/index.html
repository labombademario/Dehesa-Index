Western Greece: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Greece (Greece): agricultural industry output 1,548 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Greece reached 1,548 M EUR in 2023 (+5.3 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 76 %, animals (livestock sold) 7 %, animal products 15 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 407 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (110 thousand ha, 27 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 63.0, pigs 76.5, sheep 1,022. There are 69,290 holdings (2020) with an average area of 3.5 ha. Western Greece is at rank 5 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
