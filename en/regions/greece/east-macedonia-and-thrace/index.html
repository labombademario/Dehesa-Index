East Macedonia and Thrace: farm data and statistics

Official figures for East Macedonia and Thrace (Greece): agricultural industry output 1,063 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in East Macedonia and Thrace reached 1,063 M EUR in 2023 (−3.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 75 %, animals (livestock sold) 6 %, animal products 16 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (21 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 447 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is durum wheat (56.6 thousand ha, 13 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 67.5, pigs 64.4, sheep 231. There are 36,240 holdings (2020) with an average area of 8.5 ha. East Macedonia and Thrace is at rank 6 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
