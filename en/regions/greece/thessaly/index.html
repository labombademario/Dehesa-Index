Thessaly: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Thessaly (Greece): agricultural industry output 2,334 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Thessaly reached 2,334 M EUR in 2023 (+6.4 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 78 %, animals (livestock sold) 6 %, animal products 12 % of output; the largest single item is fruit (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 637 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is durum wheat (84.2 thousand ha, 13 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 115, pigs 111, sheep 865. There are 45,760 holdings (2020) with an average area of 7.6 ha. Thessaly is at rank 2 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
