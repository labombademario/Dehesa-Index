Crete: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Crete (Greece): agricultural industry output 1,774 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Crete reached 1,774 M EUR in 2023 (+10.8 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 75 %, animals (livestock sold) 7 %, animal products 15 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (30 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 709 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (208 thousand ha, 29 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 0.95, pigs 15.8, sheep 2,055. There are 74,130 holdings (2020) with an average area of 4.5 ha. Crete is at rank 4 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
