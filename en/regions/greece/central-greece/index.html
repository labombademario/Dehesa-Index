Central Greece: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Greece (Greece): agricultural industry output 951 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Greece reached 951 M EUR in 2023 (+1.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 73 %, animals (livestock sold) 10 %, animal products 14 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (15 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 552 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (86.4 thousand ha, 16 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 27.1, pigs 108, sheep 318. There are 54,420 holdings (2020) with an average area of 4.6 ha. Central Greece is at rank 7 of 13 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
