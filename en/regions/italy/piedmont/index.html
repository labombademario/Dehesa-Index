Piedmont: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Piedmont (Italy): agricultural industry output 4,537 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Piedmont reached 4,537 M EUR in 2023 (−1.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 52 %, animals (livestock sold) 29 %, animal products 15 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (20 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 889 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (116 thousand ha, 13 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 51,600 holdings (2020) with an average area of 17.6 ha. Piedmont is at rank 6 of 20 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
