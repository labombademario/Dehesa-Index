Northern and Western: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Northern and Western (Ireland): agricultural industry output 2,811 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Northern and Western reached 2,811 M EUR in 2024 (+10.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 22 %, animals (livestock sold) 52 %, animal products 20 % of output; the largest single item is cattle (31 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 1,518 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is barley (6.16 thousand ha, 0 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 1,551, pigs 366, sheep 1,968. There are 54,840 holdings (2023) with an average area of 29.5 ha. Northern and Western is at rank 3 of 3 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
