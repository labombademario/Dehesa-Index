Southern: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southern (Ireland): agricultural industry output 6,691 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southern reached 6,691 M EUR in 2024 (+8.0 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 19 %, animals (livestock sold) 33 %, animal products 42 % of output; the largest single item is milk (42 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 2,056 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is barley (106 thousand ha, 5 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 3,333, pigs 729, sheep 964. There are 50,410 holdings (2023) with an average area of 42.6 ha. Southern is at rank 1 of 3 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
