Extremadura: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Extremadura (Spain): agricultural industry output 3,530 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this autonomous community and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Extremadura reached 3,530 M EUR in 2023 (+9.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 56 %, animals (livestock sold) 39 %, animal products 4 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (20 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 2,890 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is olives (269 thousand ha, 9 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 64,280 holdings (2020) with an average area of 43.3 ha. Extremadura is at rank 9 of 17 for agricultural output among the autonomous communities with data.
