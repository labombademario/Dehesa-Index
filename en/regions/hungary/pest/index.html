Pest: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Pest (Hungary): agricultural industry output 641 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Pest reached 641 M EUR in 2023 (+12.3 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 56 %, animals (livestock sold) 24 %, animal products 13 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 275 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (45.1 thousand ha, 16 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 80.4, pigs 29.0, sheep 44.6. There are 11,840 holdings (2023) with an average area of 23.8 ha. Pest is at rank 7 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
