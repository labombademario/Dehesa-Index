Northern Hungary: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Northern Hungary (Hungary): agricultural industry output 925 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Northern Hungary reached 925 M EUR in 2023 (+9.2 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 72 %, animals (livestock sold) 11 %, animal products 12 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (33 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 565 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (111 thousand ha, 20 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 92.0, pigs 86.8, sheep 88.7. There are 19,960 holdings (2023) with an average area of 29.6 ha. Northern Hungary is at rank 6 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
