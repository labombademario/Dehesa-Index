Southern Transdanubia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southern Transdanubia (Hungary): agricultural industry output 1,609 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southern Transdanubia reached 1,609 M EUR in 2023 (−3.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 60 %, animals (livestock sold) 23 %, animal products 9 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (33 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 718 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is grain maize (181 thousand ha, 25 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 100, pigs 752, sheep 56.2. There are 20,970 holdings (2023) with an average area of 33.4 ha. Southern Transdanubia is at rank 3 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
