Southern Great Plain: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southern Great Plain (Hungary): agricultural industry output 2,821 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southern Great Plain reached 2,821 M EUR in 2023 (+22.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 59 %, animals (livestock sold) 25 %, animal products 10 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (24 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 1,250 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (207 thousand ha, 17 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 180, pigs 679, sheep 211. There are 52,250 holdings (2023) with an average area of 24.0 ha. Southern Great Plain is at rank 1 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
