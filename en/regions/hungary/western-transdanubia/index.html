Western Transdanubia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Transdanubia (Hungary): agricultural industry output 1,369 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Transdanubia reached 1,369 M EUR in 2023 (−4.5 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 53 %, animals (livestock sold) 27 %, animal products 14 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (28 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 542 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (106 thousand ha, 20 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 98.9, pigs 240, sheep 21.6. There are 18,700 holdings (2023) with an average area of 29.6 ha. Western Transdanubia is at rank 4 of 8 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
