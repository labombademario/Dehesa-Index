Western Finland: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Western Finland (Finland): agricultural industry output 2,135 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Western Finland reached 2,135 M EUR in 2023 (−6.5 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 42 %, animals (livestock sold) 32 %, animal products 23 % of output; the largest single item is milk (20 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 788 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is barley (136 thousand ha, 17 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 16,050 holdings (2023) with an average area of 48.6 ha. Western Finland is at rank 1 of 5 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
