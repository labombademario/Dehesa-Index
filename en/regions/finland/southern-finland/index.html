Southern Finland: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southern Finland (Finland): agricultural industry output 1,223 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southern Finland reached 1,223 M EUR in 2023 (−10.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 54 %, animals (livestock sold) 20 %, animal products 24 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (22 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 611 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (107 thousand ha, 18 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 93.5, pigs 321. There are 10,020 holdings (2023) with an average area of 61.5 ha. Southern Finland is at rank 3 of 5 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
