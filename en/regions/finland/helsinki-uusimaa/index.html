Helsinki-Uusimaa: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Helsinki-Uusimaa (Finland): agricultural industry output 248 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Helsinki-Uusimaa reached 248 M EUR in 2023 (−13.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 66 %, animals (livestock sold) 11 %, animal products 19 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (33 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 179 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (36.9 thousand ha, 21 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 2,850 holdings (2023) with an average area of 63.3 ha. Helsinki-Uusimaa is at rank 4 of 5 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
