Southeast Czechia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southeast Czechia (Czechia): agricultural industry output 1,763 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southeast Czechia reached 1,763 M EUR in 2023 (−6.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 52 %, animals (livestock sold) 18 %, animal products 24 % of output; the largest single item is milk (23 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 722 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (172 thousand ha, 24 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 9,700 holdings (2023) with an average area of 75.0 ha. Southeast Czechia is at rank 1 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
