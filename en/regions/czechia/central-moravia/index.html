Central Moravia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Moravia (Czechia): agricultural industry output 746 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Moravia reached 746 M EUR in 2023 (−9.8 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 60 %, animals (livestock sold) 12 %, animal products 26 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 395 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (78.1 thousand ha, 20 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 3,750 holdings (2023) with an average area of 105.6 ha. Central Moravia is at rank 5 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
