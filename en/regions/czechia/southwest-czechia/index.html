Southwest Czechia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Southwest Czechia (Czechia): agricultural industry output 1,311 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Southwest Czechia reached 1,311 M EUR in 2023 (−8.1 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 48 %, animals (livestock sold) 17 %, animal products 31 % of output; the largest single item is milk (28 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 753 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (135 thousand ha, 18 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 5,650 holdings (2023) with an average area of 131.4 ha. Southwest Czechia is at rank 3 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
