Central Bohemia: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Central Bohemia (Czechia): agricultural industry output 1,394 M EUR (2023). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Central Bohemia reached 1,394 M EUR in 2023 (−3.3 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 62 %, animals (livestock sold) 16 %, animal products 18 % of output; the largest single item is cereals (24 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 555 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is common wheat (160 thousand ha, 29 % of utilised agricultural area). There are 4,280 holdings (2023) with an average area of 128.4 ha. Central Bohemia is at rank 2 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
