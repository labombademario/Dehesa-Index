Alentejo: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Alentejo (Portugal): agricultural industry output 3,647 M EUR (2022). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Alentejo reached 3,647 M EUR in 2022 (+12.7 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 68 %, animals (livestock sold) 24 %, animal products 5 % of output; the largest single item is fruit (26 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 2,319 thousand ha (2022); the crop group with the most land is olives (209 thousand ha, 9 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2022, thousand head): cattle 739, pigs 936, sheep 1,436. There are 39,360 holdings (2020) with an average area of 59.7 ha. Alentejo is at rank 1 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
