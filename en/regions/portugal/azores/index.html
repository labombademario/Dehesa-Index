Azores: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Azores (Portugal): agricultural industry output 628 M EUR (2024). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Azores reached 628 M EUR in 2024 (+0.4 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 21 %, animals (livestock sold) 30 %, animal products 47 % of output; the largest single item is milk (45 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 125 thousand ha (2024); the crop group with the most land is green maize (14.1 thousand ha, 11 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2025, thousand head): cattle 268, pigs 43.6, sheep 6.06. There are 9,260 holdings (2023) with an average area of 13.0 ha. Azores is at rank 6 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
