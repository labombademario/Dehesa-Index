Centro: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Centro (Portugal): agricultural industry output 2,975 M EUR (2022). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Centro reached 2,975 M EUR in 2022 (+8.4 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 48 %, animals (livestock sold) 36 %, animal products 13 % of output; the largest single item is poultry (21 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 637 thousand ha (2022); the crop group with the most land is olives (77.9 thousand ha, 12 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2022, thousand head): cattle 189, pigs 880, sheep 471. There are 97,590 holdings (2020) with an average area of 6.5 ha. Centro is at rank 2 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
