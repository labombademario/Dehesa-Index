Lisbon Metropolitan Area: farm data and statistics

Official figures for Lisbon Metropolitan Area (Portugal): agricultural industry output 661 M EUR (2022). This page gathers the farm data published for this region and links to the interactive profile.

In brief

Agricultural industry output in Lisbon Metropolitan Area reached 661 M EUR in 2022 (+8.6 % on the previous year). By origin, crop output 69 %, animals (livestock sold) 22 %, animal products 6 % of output; the largest single item is vegetables and horticulture (36 %). Utilised agricultural area totals 78.8 thousand ha (2022); the crop group with the most land is vines (grapes) (8.07 thousand ha, 10 % of utilised agricultural area). Livestock (2022, thousand head): cattle 59.1, pigs 237, sheep 39.8. There are 6,530 holdings (2020) with an average area of 13.9 ha. Lisbon Metropolitan Area is at rank 5 of 7 for agricultural output among the regions with data.
