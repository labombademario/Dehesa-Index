What each price measures

  • European Commission weekly price: reported by member states by product type and stage of sale (farm-gate, packing-house or retailer buying price), in euro per kilo, excluding VAT.
  • Price received by farmers in Spain (MAPA): monthly average of what the grower is paid for the product at the farm, in euro per kilo.
  • UK wholesale price (Defra): weekly average wholesale selling price of home-grown produce, in pounds per kilo.
