Dairy Data

  • Updated: 10/15/2026
  • Contact: Adriana Valcu-Lisman or Angel Terán
  • Dairy Data
    • Documentation

Dairy data includes summary statistics, estimates, and compilations of economic data concerning the U.S. dairy industry. Dairy data allows users to understand and analyze important supply and demand factors affecting the U.S. dairy industry. Dairy data is updated monthly, quarterly, and annually. It uses data from primary sources, federal and nonfederal, to provide added value data regarding U.S. dairy industry.

See the Documentation page for information on how the data were assembled, sources, and recent and upcoming changes to the data.
