What is the OpenMB Information and Data Use Licence? The OpenMB Information and Data Use License is a legal framework that facilitates the re-use of a wide range of Manitoba government information and data, free of charge.&nbsp;The OpenMB License is available here . The Manitoba government adopted this licence to enable individuals an organizations to re-use publicly available information and data under a common open licence. The OpenMB License makes clear that most material covered by Crown copyright can be copied, modified, published, translated, adapted, distributed or otherwise used, without seeking additional permissions from the government. What is not covered under the OpenMB Licence? Most government information and data that is available publicly is covered under the licence, with a few important exceptions: &nbsp; The use of official government symbols is not permitted under this licence. For example, the Manitoba logo, other logos and visual identities created by government, the coat of arms and the augmented coat of arms, the Manitoba Tartan and the Golden Boy are all symbols that require the express permission of the government before they can be used by anyone. Permission can be sought by contacting the King&#39;s Printer for Manitoba Information that belongs to a third-party is governed by that third-party and can&#39;t be licensed by the government. You would need to seek the permission of the third-party originator of that material. Any information that includes personal information cannot be used without proper permission. &nbsp; Why did Manitoba adopt the OpenMB Information and Data Use Licence?
