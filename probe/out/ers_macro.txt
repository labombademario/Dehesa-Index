Visit the Newsroom Events Trending Topics Contact ERS Press Office Subject Specialists Amber Waves Calendar About Careers Contact Us Search Breadcrumb Home Data Products International Macroeconomic Data Set International Macroeconomic Data Set Updated: 5/27/2026 Contact: Wendy Zeng or William Johnson In this section International Macroeconomic Data Set Documentation The Economic Research Service’s (ERS) International Macroeconomic Data Set provides annual historical and projected data for 181 countries that account for more than 99 percent of the global economy. These data and projections are assembled explicitly to serve as underlying assumptions for the annual USDA agricultural supply and demand projections, which provide a 10-year outlook on U.S. and global agriculture. The macroeconomic projections describe the long-term, 10-year scenario that is used as a benchmark for analyzing the impacts of alternative scenarios and macroeconomic shocks.
